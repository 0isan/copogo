How It Works

Simple steps to buy and sell your UPI coupons

For Sellers

1

List Your Coupon

Take a photo of your coupon or upload the details, set a price, and list it on CopoGo.

2

Wait for Buyer

Buyers browse and select your coupon. You'll be notified when someone makes a purchase.

3

Get Paid

Receive payment directly to your UPI ID once the buyer confirms the coupon is valid.

For Buyers

1

Find a Coupon

Browse available coupons by category, app, or discount value to find what you need.

2

Make Payment

Purchase the coupon at a discounted price, which is held securely until you verify the coupon.

3

Use the Coupon

Receive and use the coupon in your UPI app. Confirm receipt to release payment to the seller.
